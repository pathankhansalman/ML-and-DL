# Target PhD Supervisors & Institutions: Causal Abstractions & Robust Causal ML

> **Research Focus:** Structural Causal Models (SCMs), Formal Causal Abstractions, Interventionally Consistent Surrogates (ICS4CSM), and Distributionally Robust Optimization (DRO / DiRoCA).

---

## 🇬🇧 United Kingdom

### 1. University of Warwick
* **Key Faculty:** **Prof. Theodoros Damoulas**
  * **Lab:** Warwick Machine Learning Group & The Alan Turing Institute
  * **Alignment:** Primary co-author of *ICS4CSM* (NeurIPS 2024) and *DiRoCA* (ICML 2025). Direct intellectual fit for causal abstractions, surrogate digital twins, and distributionally robust causal models.
  * **Key Researchers / Postdocs:** Yorgos Felekis (PhD / lead author on DiRoCA and Causal Optimal Transport of Abstractions).
  * **Part-Time PhD:** **Yes, officially offered** (typically 5–7 years). Highly flexible for industry/research balance.

### 2. University College London (UCL)
* **Key Faculty:** **Prof. Ricardo Silva** & **Dr. Sander Beckers**
  * **Lab:** Causality in Healthcare AI Hub (Department of Statistical Science / Computer Science).
  * **Alignment:** 
    * **Ricardo Silva:** Pioneer in causal machine learning and graphical models.
    * **Sander Beckers:** Co-author of the foundational *Beckers & Halpern (2019/2020)* causal abstraction framework.
  * **Part-Time PhD:** **Yes, offered** (typically 5 years). Requires existing UK residency/work entitlement as UKVI does not sponsor student visas for part-time study.

### 3. University of Cambridge
* **Key Faculty:** **Prof. Mihaela van der Schaar**
  * **Lab:** Cambridge Centre for AI in Medicine (CCAIM).
  * **Alignment:** Causal inference for healthcare, synthetic data, causal digital twins, and individualized treatment effects (ITE).
  * **Part-Time PhD:** Available on a case-by-case basis subject to departmental and supervisor approval.

## 🇸🇬 Singapore

### 1. Nanyang Technological University (NTU)
* **Key Faculty:**
  * **Prof. Rob Cornish** (College of Computing and Data Science - CCDS): Causal inference, generative models, and uncertainty quantification.
  * **Prof. Zhang Hanwang** (CCDS): Causal representation learning, invariant representations, and counterfactual multimodal AI.
  * **Prof. Dino Sejdinovic** (CCDS / Oxford affiliate): Kernel methods and statistical foundations of causal inference.

### 2. National University of Singapore (NUS)
* **Key Faculty:**
  * **Dr. Davin Choo** (School of Computing): Causal DAG algorithms and statistical ML.
  * **Dr. Yao Zhang** (Dept. of Statistics and Data Science): Causal inference and predictive machine learning.
  * **Prof. Ke-Wei Huang** (School of Computing / DISA): Causal machine learning and structural econometrics.

### 3. Singapore Part-Time PhD Policy Notice:
* Both NUS and NTU offer part-time PhD routes, but **Singapore ICA does not issue Student's Passes (visas) for part-time study**.
* To qualify for part-time PhD enrollment in Singapore, candidates must generally be:
  1. Singapore Citizens or Permanent Residents (PRs), OR
  2. Holding an Employment Pass (EP) working locally at a Singapore company, university lab, or research institute (e.g., A*STAR).
